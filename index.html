Full-stack · AI · Flagship

MindLink

An AI-powered mental wellness companion I founded and built solo. Its assistant, Mia, replies using your real mood, sleep and journal data.

MindLink is a mobile wellness app built around Mia, an AI companion powered by OpenAI GPT-4o. Instead of generic advice, Mia answers using what the person has actually logged: their mood, their sleep and their journal entries.

  • Ten screens: Welcome, Onboarding, Login, Home, Chat, Sleep, Journal, Mood Tracker, Medication and Profile, in a consistent dark forest-green design.
  • Backend: a Node.js and Express API on MongoDB Atlas, with JWT authentication and refresh-token rotation.
  • AI layer: a dedicated Mia service that turns the user's data into context for GPT-4o.
  • Reminders: Expo push notifications and a scheduler service for check-ins and medication reminders.
  • Quality: Jest tests on the backend.

Next on the roadmap: a Play Store release, hospital integration, and deeper sleep and wellness modules.
